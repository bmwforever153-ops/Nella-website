Essential Pages for the Main Menu

- Home Page: The central landing spot that gives a quick overview of your business, location, and best work.
- Services and Pricing Page: A dedicated space breaking down your exact tiers, package details, and vehicle sizing rates.
- Gallery / Portfolio Page: A visual library showing off your best transformations, before-and-afters, and high-end vehicle details.
- About Us Page: The story of who you are, your certifications, your experience, and why customers should trust you with their cars.
- Contact / Booking Page: The final destination containing your phone number, email, service area map, and a booking form.

Essential Sections for the Home Page

- Hero Section: The top header featuring a premium car background image, a clear headline of what you do, your service city, and a "Book Now" button.
- Value Proposition Section: Three or four quick icons explaining what makes you different, such as being fully insured, using eco-friendly products, or offering mobile convenience.
- Featured Packages Section: A side-by-side preview grid of your three most popular packages (e.g., Full Interior, Exterior Refresh, Ceramic Protection) with a "View All Services" link.
- Before and After Showcase: An interactive sliding photo module or a clean grid showing dramatic cleanups of trashed interiors or swirled paint.
- Customer Reviews Grid: A block of embedded five-star Google or Facebook reviews with the names and vehicle models of real clients.
- Service Area Map: A visual map graphic or clear text listing showing exactly which zip codes, suburbs, or cities you travel to or operate in.
- Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ): A dropdown accordion answering common questions about rain policies, detailing times, and how customers should prepare their cars.
- Footer Section: The very bottom of the page containing links to your social media, business hours, privacy policy, and copyright information.
